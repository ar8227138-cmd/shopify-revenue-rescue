Shopify Revenue Rescue

AI-POWERED e-commerce data analytics project for identifying Shopify revenue leaks

📌 Project Overview

Shopify and other e-commerce businesses can generate strong sales while still losing money through inefficient advertising spend, high customer acquisition costs, and poor cost-to-revenue efficiency.

Shopify Revenue Rescue is a business-focused data analytics project designed to identify these potential revenue leaks and provide practical recommendations for improving revenue efficiency.

The project follows this workflow:

Raw Data → Analysis → Revenue Leak Detection → Recovery Opportunity → Recommendations

---

🎯 Business Problem

The business needs to understand:

- Where advertising spending becomes inefficient
- Where revenue leaks are occurring
- Whether advertising continues during periods of weak or zero sales
- Where customer acquisition costs become too high
- How potential revenue recovery opportunities can be identified

---

🔍 Key Analysis Areas

1. High Customer Acquisition Cost

The analysis examines advertising spending in relation to sales revenue to identify periods where customer acquisition becomes inefficient.

2. Revenue Leak Detection

The dataset is analyzed to identify potential revenue leaks, including:

- Advertising spend exceeding generated revenue
- Advertising spend continuing during periods of zero sales
- High revenue periods with inefficient advertising costs

3. Revenue Recovery Plan

Based on the identified issues, the project provides practical recommendations to reduce inefficient spending and improve revenue performance.

---

📊 Dataset

The project uses an e-commerce sales and advertising dataset containing information needed to evaluate revenue and advertising performance.

Dataset file:

"shopify_revenue_rescue_dataset.xlsx"

The analysis and findings are based on the data contained in this file.

---

🧠 Analytical Methodology

The project follows these steps:

1. Inspect the dataset
2. Validate and clean the data
3. Analyze sales and advertising performance
4. Calculate relevant business KPIs
5. Identify revenue leaks
6. Evaluate potential recovery opportunities
7. Develop business recommendations
8. Present the findings in a client-ready report

---

💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying situations where:

- Advertising costs are disproportionately high compared with revenue
- Advertising continues despite weak or zero sales
- Strong revenue does not necessarily mean efficient spending
- Monitoring advertising performance can help identify potential revenue leaks

The detailed findings are presented in the project report.

---

💰 Revenue Recovery Opportunity

The project evaluates the potential revenue recovery opportunity associated with the identified revenue leaks.

The recovery calculation is documented in the accompanying report and should be interpreted together with the underlying dataset.

📈 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, an e-commerce business should:

1. Monitor advertising spend against generated revenue.
2. Review campaigns producing little or no revenue.
3. Establish performance thresholds for advertising campaigns.
4. Investigate periods with unusually high acquisition costs.
5. Monitor revenue leaks regularly.
6. Use data-driven decisions to improve advertising efficiency.
